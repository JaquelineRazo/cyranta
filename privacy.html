Privacy Notice

How CYRANTA handles the information you share through this site. Last updated October 20, 2026.

Leer en español ↓

Who is responsible

Jaqueline Razo, an individual who operates CYRANTA, based in Mexico City, Mexico. For anything related to your information, write to cyranta@razzobusiness.com.

What we collect

Only what you choose to send us:

  • Through the request form: your name, work email, organization (optional), the type of organization and the service you select, your message, and how you found the site (for example, a campaign tag in the link).
  • By email: whatever you include in your message.
  • If you book a call: the details the booking tool asks for, such as your name, email and what you would like to talk about.

The hosting provider keeps standard technical logs (such as IP address and browser) to operate and secure the site. We also collect anonymous, aggregated statistics (pages visited, how visitors arrived, device type and country) through Umami, an analytics service that does not use cookies or identify individual visitors.

This form is not for sharing datasets or confidential files. We only review client data after signing an NDA.

Why we use it

To reply to you, follow up on the conversation you started, prepare a Snapshot if you request one, and keep a record of it. We do not sell your information, and we will not add you to any marketing list without your explicit permission.

Who processes it for us

  • Cloudflare, which runs the service that receives the form, and Resend, which delivers it to our inbox.
  • Google Workspace, for email and calendar.
  • TidyCal, for booking calls.
  • GitHub Pages, which hosts this site, and Google Fonts and jsDelivr, which deliver its fonts and code libraries.
  • Umami, for anonymous, cookie-free site statistics.

Several of them are located in the United States, so your information may be transferred and stored outside your country.

How long we keep it

For as long as our conversation or working relationship lasts, and up to 24 months after our last contact. After that, we delete it.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your information, or object to how it is used, by writing to cyranta@razzobusiness.com. We will respond as soon as possible and within the time required by applicable law.

Cookies

This site does not use advertising or tracking cookies, and its statistics tool (Umami) works without cookies. If that changes, this notice will be updated first.

Changes

Any change to this notice will be published on this page with a new date.
