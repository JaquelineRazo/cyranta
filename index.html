┤ FAQ ├

Questions, answered.

Is the Snapshot really free?

Yes. It works like a model demo: you test the data before you decide to buy.

Yes. Each dataset is licensed to a single buyer.

Yes. Tell us the industry, the fields and the use case, and we'll scope it after the Snapshot.

Parquet, JSONL or CSV, or the format your pipeline already uses.
